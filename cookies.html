Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Acquileadz uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember your preferences and understand how you use them.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the site to function properly. They enable core features like security, form processing, and basic navigation. You cannot opt out of essential cookies while using the site.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site. These cookies are disabled by default and only activated if you choose "Accept All" in our cookie banner. Analytics data helps us improve the site experience.

Your Choices

When you first visit our site, you'll see a cookie banner asking for your preference. You can choose to accept all cookies or use essential cookies only. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site.

Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can block or delete cookies, though this may affect site functionality. Check your browser's help section for specific instructions.

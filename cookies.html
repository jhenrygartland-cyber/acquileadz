Cookie Policy

We explain which cookies we use, why, and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help websites function and improve your experience. We use them to keep the site secure and to understand performance when you consent.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential: Required for site operation, security, and form submission. These cannot be turned off in our systems.
  • Analytics (optional): Help us understand site usage. These load only if you select "Accept all cookies."

Managing Your Choices

Use the cookie banner on any page to accept all cookies or restrict to essential only. You can revisit your choice by clearing site cookies or adjusting browser settings. Analytics remain off by default until you opt in.

Third-Party Cookies

If we enable analytics or embed content from other providers, those services may place their own cookies. We will disclose the providers and purposes before enabling them.

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making our website accessible to everyone.

Our Commitment

Acquileadz is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards to our website.

Conformance Goal

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.

What We're Doing

  • Using semantic HTML to provide meaningful structure
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds
  • Providing text alternatives for non-text content
  • Making all functionality available via keyboard navigation
  • Designing forms with clear labels and error messages
  • Testing with assistive technologies including screen readers
  • Respecting user preferences for reduced motion

Testing Approach

We test our website using a combination of automated tools and manual testing. This includes testing with screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, and browser accessibility features. We review our site periodically to identify and address accessibility issues.

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and resolve these issues. If you encounter any barriers, please let us know so we can address them promptly.

Request Accommodations

If you need assistance accessing any content or functionality on our website, or if you have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us:

Email: accessibility@acquileadz.com

When contacting us, please include:

  • The web page URL where you encountered the issue
  • A description of the accessibility barrier
  • The assistive technology you use (if applicable)
  • Your preferred method of contact

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days and will work with you to provide the information or functionality you need.

Third-Party Content

Our website may include content from third-party sources. While we choose partners who share our commitment to accessibility, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content. We encourage you to contact us if you encounter any issues.

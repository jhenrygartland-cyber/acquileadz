Privacy Policy

We explain what we collect, why, and the rights you have over your data. We comply with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable laws.

What We Collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, company, and message when you submit our forms.
  • Usage data: basic device and page analytics when enabled by your consent.
  • Technical data: essential cookies to keep the site and forms secure.

How We Use Your Data

  • Responding to inquiries and scheduling calls.
  • Providing services you request and maintaining our relationship.
  • Improving site performance and security.
  • Complying with legal obligations and preventing fraud.

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run the site (e.g., form processor, hosting) under confidentiality obligations.

Lawful Bases (GDPR)

  • Consent: for optional analytics and marketing communications.
  • Contract: to provide requested services and respond to your inquiries.
  • Legitimate interest: to secure our site and prevent abuse.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable laws and enforcement requests.

Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (does not affect prior lawful processing).
  • File a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California residents also have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of certain sharing under the CCPA/CPRA. We honor authorized agent requests when properly verified.

Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to operate the site and process forms. Optional analytics cookies are off by default and only load if you choose “Accept all cookies.” You can change your choice anytime using the cookie controls on each page.

Data Retention

We keep contact form submissions for as long as needed to respond and maintain our business relationship, then delete or anonymize them unless we must retain them for legal, security, or audit reasons.

Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and vendor due diligence to protect your data. No method is 100% secure; please reach out if you have concerns.

Third-Party Services

We use Web3Forms to process contact form submissions and may use hosting or analytics providers. These vendors are bound by agreements to protect your information and use it only for our instructions.

Contact

Email privacy@acquileadz.com for any privacy request. We aim to respond within 30 days.

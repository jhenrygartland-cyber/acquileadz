Common Questions

How quickly will I see results?

Most contractors see their first booked appointments within 2 weeks of launch. Significant pipeline growth typically happens in the first 60–90 days as we optimize targeting and follow-up sequences.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

We require a 3-month initial commitment — that's the minimum time needed to build, launch, and optimize your system. After that, you can continue month-to-month or cancel anytime.

What if I already have a CRM?

No problem. We can work with your existing CRM or set up a new one — whatever makes sense for your business. Our systems integrate with most major platforms.

How is this different from buying leads?

Lead vendors sell you names. We build you a system. You own your campaigns, your data, and your pipeline. We don't just hand you leads — we nurture them, qualify them, and book them on your calendar.

What types of contractors do you work with?

We specialize in home service contractors: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, painting, landscaping, and similar trades. If you serve homeowners and want more booked jobs, we can help.

What's included in the strategy call?

We'll review your current marketing, discuss your goals, and map out a plan to hit them. You'll leave with actionable insights — whether you hire us or not. No pitch, no pressure.
